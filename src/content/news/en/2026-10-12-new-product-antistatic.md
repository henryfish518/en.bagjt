We have expanded our product range and put **TYPE-D antistatic bulk bags** into regular production — specialised packaging for the safe handling of combustible and explosive powders.

## Where TYPE-D bulk bags are used

Antistatic FIBC containers are designed for environments where there is a risk of ignition from static discharge:

- Coal dust and coke
- Sulphur and sulphur powder
- Mineral fertilizers and blended fertilizer mixes
- Powdered chemicals and pigments
- Powdered food products (flour, milk powder, cocoa)

## Technical specification

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Safety factor | 6:1 |
| Load capacity | 1000–2000 kg |
| Antistatic fabric | surface resistance ≤ 10⁸ Ω |
| Standard sizes | 90×90×110, 100×100×120 cm |
| Available liners | PE liner, paper liner |
| Colour | White / black |

## What TYPE-D offers over ordinary bulk bags

TYPE-D does not require grounding during loading and unloading — the antistatic fabric dissipates the charge without a conductive connection. This is critical for mobile sites, field conditions and workshops without a permanent grounding circuit.

> All TYPE-D bulk bags are certified to UN standards and are supplied with test protocols confirming compliance with ADR requirements (transport of dangerous goods).
