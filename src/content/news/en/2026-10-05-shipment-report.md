In the first week of October we dispatched 6 containers of FIBC bulk bags to customers in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Egypt.

## October deliveries

| Category | Quantity | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Universal bulk bags | 2 containers | Chemical raw materials |
| Food-grade bulk bags | 2 containers | Grain, flour and dates |
| Bulk bags with liner | 1 container | Mineral powders |
| TYPE-C antistatic | 1 container | Pigments |

## How we plan deliveries

1. **Production reservation** — line capacity is booked in advance for contract batches;
2. **Three-stage inspection** — fabric check, sewing control and final inspection before packing;
3. **Palletized packing** — 100 pcs. per pallet, moisture-proof wrapping, customer-specified marking.

## Document package

Every shipment to the Gulf and the wider MENA region includes:

- invoice and packing list;
- batch test protocol;
- ISO 9001 quality certificates (ISO 22000 for food grade);
- product data sheet.

> Destinations this month: Jebel Ali (UAE), Dammam (Saudi Arabia) and Alexandria (Egypt). Regular shipments are the result of long-term contracts — we reserve production capacity for customers with monthly volumes, so contact us to discuss a delivery schedule.

The next shipment report will follow after the November dispatches.
